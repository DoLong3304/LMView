# Cryptocurrency Market Structure

## Exchange Types

### Centralized Exchanges (CEX)
- Binance, OKX, Coinbase, Kraken, etc.
- Order book-based matching engines.
- Custodial: exchange holds your funds.
- Higher liquidity for major pairs.
- Subject to regulation and KYC requirements.

### Decentralized Exchanges (DEX)
- Uniswap, SushiSwap, dYdX, etc.
- Automated Market Maker (AMM) or on-chain order books.
- Non-custodial: users control their keys.
- May have lower liquidity and higher slippage.

## Order Types

### Market Orders
Execute immediately at the best available price. May experience slippage in low-liquidity markets.

### Limit Orders
Execute only at a specified price or better. No slippage, but may not fill.

### Stop-Loss Orders
Trigger a market or limit order when price reaches a specified level. Used for risk management.

### Take-Profit Orders
Automatically close a position when target price is reached. Used to lock in profits.

## Market Dynamics

### Liquidity
- Higher liquidity = tighter spreads and less slippage.
- Major pairs (BTC/USDT, ETH/USDT) have the highest liquidity.
- Liquidity varies by exchange and time of day.
- Low liquidity can lead to flash crashes or manipulation.

### Volatility
- Crypto markets are significantly more volatile than traditional markets.
- Volatility is highest during major news events, regulatory announcements, and market opens/closes in traditional markets.
- High volatility creates both opportunities and risks.

### Market Cycles
- **Accumulation**: Smart money buys at low prices after a downtrend.
- **Markup**: Price begins trending upward, attracting more buyers.
- **Distribution**: Smart money sells at high prices to retail buyers.
- **Markdown**: Price declines as selling pressure overwhelms buying.

### Correlation
- BTC dominance affects altcoin behavior.
- When BTC dominance rises, altcoins often underperform.
- Macro factors (interest rates, stock markets) increasingly affect crypto.

## Trading Pairs
- **Quote currency**: Usually USDT, USDC, BTC, or ETH.
- **Base currency**: The asset being traded.
- Example: BTCUSDT means Bitcoin priced in USDT.

## Key Metrics
- **Market Cap**: Price × Circulating Supply.
- **Trading Volume**: Total value traded in a period.
- **Bid-Ask Spread**: Difference between highest bid and lowest ask.
- **Open Interest**: Total outstanding derivative contracts.
- **Funding Rate**: Periodic payments between long/short in perpetual futures.
