# Bilingual Crypto Trading Glossary (English / Vietnamese)

## Market Terms

| English | Vietnamese | Definition |
|---------|-----------|------------|
| Bull market | Thị trường tăng giá | Period of rising prices |
| Bear market | Thị trường giảm giá | Period of falling prices |
| Volatility | Biến động | Degree of price fluctuation |
| Liquidity | Thanh khoản | Ease of buying/selling without impacting price |
| Market cap | Vốn hóa thị trường | Total market value of a cryptocurrency |
| Volume | Khối lượng giao dịch | Amount traded in a period |
| Spread | Chênh lệch giá | Difference between bid and ask prices |
| Slippage | Trượt giá | Difference between expected and executed price |
| Whale | Cá voi | Large holder who can influence market |

## Trading Terms

| English | Vietnamese | Definition |
|---------|-----------|------------|
| Long position | Vị thế mua | Buying with expectation of price increase |
| Short position | Vị thế bán | Selling with expectation of price decrease |
| Stop-loss | Cắt lỗ | Order to limit losses |
| Take-profit | Chốt lời | Order to lock in profits |
| Leverage | Đòn bẩy | Using borrowed funds to increase position size |
| Margin | Ký quỹ | Collateral required for leveraged trading |
| DYOR | Tự nghiên cứu | Do Your Own Research |
| FOMO | Sợ bỏ lỡ cơ hội | Fear Of Missing Out |
| FUD | Sợ hãi, không chắc chắn, nghi ngờ | Fear, Uncertainty, Doubt |

## Technical Analysis Terms

| English | Vietnamese | Definition |
|---------|-----------|------------|
| Support | Hỗ trợ | Price level where buying pressure prevents decline |
| Resistance | Kháng cự | Price level where selling pressure prevents advance |
| Trend | Xu hướng | General direction of price movement |
| Breakout | Phá vỡ | Price moving beyond support/resistance |
| Reversal | Đảo chiều | Change in trend direction |
| Consolidation | Tích lũy | Period of sideways price movement |
| Divergence | Phân kỳ | When indicator and price move in opposite directions |
| Overbought | Quá mua | Asset may be overvalued, potential for decline |
| Oversold | Quá bán | Asset may be undervalued, potential for rise |
| Candlestick | Nến | Chart element showing OHLC data |
| Moving average | Đường trung bình động | Average price over a specific period |
