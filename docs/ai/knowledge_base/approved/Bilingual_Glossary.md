# Bilingual Crypto and Trading Glossary (English/Vietnamese)

> **Metadata**: `review_status: approved` | `allowed_for_rag: true` | `internal_only: false`
> **Version scope**: 0.25.x | **Last reviewed**: 2026-06-16

## A
- **Ask Price / Giá Bán**: The lowest price a seller is willing to accept for an asset.
- **Asset / Tài Sản**: Anything of value that can be traded, such as a cryptocurrency or token.

## B
- **Bear Market / Thị Trường Gấu**: A market condition where prices are falling or are expected to fall.
- **Bid Price / Giá Mua**: The highest price a buyer is willing to pay for an asset.
- **Bull Market / Thị Trường Bò**: A market condition where prices are rising or are expected to rise.

## C
- **Candlestick / Nến**: A type of price chart that shows the high, low, open, and close prices of an asset for a specific period.
- **Crosshair / Con Trỏ Chữ Thập**: A tool on the chart to pinpoint exact price and time values.

## D
- **Depth / Độ Sâu (Sổ Lệnh)**: The amount of liquidity (buy and sell orders) available in the order book.
- **Derivatives / Phái Sinh**: Financial instruments whose value is derived from an underlying asset.

## F
- **Funding Rate / Phí Cấp Vốn**: Periodic payments exchanged between long and short traders in perpetual futures contracts to keep the contract price close to the spot price.

## I
- **Indicator / Chỉ Báo**: Mathematical calculations based on price, volume, or open interest used to forecast market trends (e.g., RSI, MACD).

## L
- **Leverage / Đòn Bẩy**: Using borrowed funds to increase a trading position's potential return (and risk).
- **Liquidation / Thanh Lý**: The forceful closure of a trader's leveraged position due to insufficient margin.
- **Long Position / Lệnh Mua**: Buying an asset with the expectation that its price will rise.

## O
- **Order Book / Sổ Lệnh**: A digital list of outstanding buy and sell orders for a specific asset, organized by price level.

## R
- **Resistance / Kháng Cự**: A price level where an asset has historically had difficulty rising above.

## S
- **Short Position / Lệnh Bán**: Selling a borrowed asset with the expectation that its price will fall, intending to buy it back at a lower price.
- **Slippage / Trượt Giá**: The difference between the expected price of a trade and the price at which the trade is executed.
- **Spot Market / Thị Trường Giao Ngay**: A public financial market in which financial instruments or commodities are traded for immediate delivery.
- **Support / Hỗ Trợ**: A price level where an asset has historically had difficulty falling below.

## T
- **Timeframe / Khung Thời Gian**: The duration represented by a single data point on a chart (e.g., 1m, 1h, 1d).
- **Trend Line / Đường Xu Hướng**: A line drawn on a chart to identify the direction of a market.

## V
- **Volatility / Biến Động**: A statistical measure of the dispersion of returns for a given security or market index.
- **Volume / Khối Lượng**: The number of shares or contracts traded in a security or an entire market during a given period.
