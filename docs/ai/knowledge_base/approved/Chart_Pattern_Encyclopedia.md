# Chart Pattern Encyclopedia

## Head and Shoulders (H&S)

### Description
A reversal pattern consisting of three peaks: a higher left shoulder, a higher head, and a lower right shoulder.

### Identification Rules
- Left shoulder: price rises to a peak, declines
- Head: price rises above the first peak, declines to around the same level as the left shoulder trough
- Right shoulder: price rises but fails to reach the head height, declines
- Neckline: drawn connecting the two troughs. A breakdown below the neckline confirms the pattern

### Volume Confirmation
- Volume should be highest on the left shoulder
- Volume on the head should be lower
- Volume on the right shoulder should be lowest
- Volume spike on neckline breakdown confirms validity

### Trading Implications
- Price target = distance from head to neckline, projected downward from breakdown point
- Inverted H&S signals bullish reversal

## Double Top / Double Bottom

### Description
- Double Top: two peaks at roughly the same price level, signaling bearish reversal
- Double Bottom: two troughs at roughly the same price level, signaling bullish reversal

### Identification Rules
- Peaks/troughs should be within 1-3% of each other
- Trough between peaks (for double top) should be at least 10% retracement
- Volume on second peak should be lower than first
- Breakout below/above the neckline confirms

## Triangles (Ascending, Descending, Symmetrical)

### Ascending Triangle
- Flat resistance top, rising support bottom
- Bullish continuation pattern (typically)
- Measured move = height of the triangle projected from breakout

### Descending Triangle
- Flat support bottom, declining resistance top
- Bearish continuation pattern (typically)

### Symmetrical Triangle
- Converging trendlines with declining volatility
- Breakout can go either way; direction determines bias
- Entry on volume confirmation of breakout direction

---

## Flags and Pennants

- Flag: rectangular consolidation after a sharp move, slanted against the trend
- Pennant: small symmetrical triangle after a sharp move
- Both are continuation patterns with short duration (1-3 weeks)
- Entry on breakout in the prior trend direction with volume

---

## Cup and Handle

- Cup: U-shaped rounding bottom (usually 1-6 months)
- Handle: short consolidation at the top-right of the cup (1-4 weeks)
- Breakout above handle resistance triggers entry
- Price target = depth of cup projected upward from breakout
- Volume should dry up during the handle and spike on breakout
