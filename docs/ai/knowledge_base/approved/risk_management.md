# Risk Management for Cryptocurrency Trading

## Core Principles

### Never Risk More Than You Can Afford to Lose
Cryptocurrency markets are highly volatile. Only invest money you can afford to lose entirely.

### Position Sizing
- **Fixed percentage**: Risk 1-2% of total portfolio per trade.
- **Kelly Criterion**: Mathematical approach to optimal position size.
- Smaller positions allow for more trades and better diversification.

### Stop-Loss Placement
- Place stops at logical levels (below support, above resistance).
- Use ATR-based stops for volatility-adjusted distances.
- Never move a stop-loss further from entry to avoid taking a loss.

### Risk-Reward Ratio
- Aim for at least 1:2 risk-reward ratio.
- A 1:3 ratio means potential profit is 3x the potential loss.
- Win rate and R:R together determine profitability.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Over-leveraging**: Using excessive leverage amplifies both gains and losses.
2. **No stop-loss**: Trading without predefined exit points.
3. **Revenge trading**: Making impulsive trades after a loss.
4. **FOMO (Fear of Missing Out)**: Chasing pumps at the top.
5. **Ignoring fees**: Trading fees, funding rates, and slippage eat into profits.
6. **Concentration risk**: Putting too much into a single asset.

## Portfolio Management

### Diversification
- Spread investments across multiple assets.
- Consider different sectors (DeFi, L1, L2, gaming, etc.).
- Include both large-cap and small-cap positions.

### Rebalancing
- Periodically adjust allocations back to target weights.
- Take profits from outperformers, add to underperformers.

### Dollar-Cost Averaging (DCA)
- Invest fixed amounts at regular intervals.
- Reduces impact of volatility on average entry price.
- Effective for long-term accumulation.
