# Technical Analysis Fundamentals

## Moving Averages

### Simple Moving Average (SMA)
The SMA calculates the average price over a specific number of periods. Common periods are 20 (short-term), 50 (medium-term), and 200 (long-term).

- **SMA 20**: Short-term trend indicator. Price above SMA 20 suggests short-term bullish trend.
- **SMA 50**: Medium-term trend. Often used for swing trading signals.
- **SMA 200**: Long-term trend. Widely watched by institutional traders.
- **Golden Cross**: SMA 50 crosses above SMA 200 — traditionally bullish signal.
- **Death Cross**: SMA 50 crosses below SMA 200 — traditionally bearish signal.

### Exponential Moving Average (EMA)
EMA gives more weight to recent prices, making it more responsive than SMA.

- **EMA 12 and EMA 26**: Used in MACD calculation.
- **EMA 50**: Popular medium-term trend filter.
- EMA reacts faster to price changes than SMA of the same period.

## Momentum Indicators

### RSI (Relative Strength Index)
RSI measures the speed and magnitude of price movements on a scale of 0-100.

- **Above 70**: Generally considered overbought — price may be due for a pullback.
- **Below 30**: Generally considered oversold — price may be due for a bounce.
- **RSI Divergence**: When price makes new highs but RSI makes lower highs, it may signal weakening momentum.
- Default period: 14.

### MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)
MACD shows the relationship between two EMAs (typically 12 and 26 period).

- **MACD Line**: EMA 12 minus EMA 26.
- **Signal Line**: 9-period EMA of the MACD line.
- **Histogram**: MACD line minus Signal line.
- **Bullish signal**: MACD crosses above signal line.
- **Bearish signal**: MACD crosses below signal line.

### Stochastic Oscillator
Compares closing price to the price range over a set period.

- **Above 80**: Overbought territory.
- **Below 20**: Oversold territory.
- **%K and %D crossovers**: Generate buy/sell signals.

## Volatility Indicators

### Bollinger Bands
Three bands: middle (SMA 20), upper (SMA + 2σ), lower (SMA - 2σ).

- **Squeeze**: Bands narrow, indicating low volatility — often precedes a big move.
- **Breakout**: Price closes outside the bands — potential trend continuation.
- **Mean reversion**: Price tends to return to the middle band.

### ATR (Average True Range)
Measures market volatility by analyzing the range of price movement.

- Higher ATR = more volatile market.
- Used for setting stop-loss distances.
- Does not indicate direction, only volatility.

## Volume Analysis

### VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price)
Average price weighted by volume. Resets daily.

- Price above VWAP: Buyers are in control.
- Price below VWAP: Sellers are in control.
- Institutional traders often use VWAP as a benchmark.

### Volume MA
Moving average applied to volume bars.

- Volume above its MA suggests increased interest.
- Volume below its MA suggests decreased interest.
- Volume should confirm price moves for stronger signals.

## Trend Indicators

### Ichimoku Cloud
A comprehensive indicator that shows support/resistance, trend direction, and momentum.

- **Tenkan-sen (Conversion Line)**: 9-period midpoint.
- **Kijun-sen (Base Line)**: 26-period midpoint.
- **Senkou Span A**: Midpoint of Tenkan and Kijun, plotted 26 periods ahead.
- **Senkou Span B**: 52-period midpoint, plotted 26 periods ahead.
- **Cloud (Kumo)**: Area between Senkou Span A and B.
- Price above cloud: Bullish. Price below cloud: Bearish. Price in cloud: Neutral.

### Supertrend
Trend-following indicator based on ATR.

- Green Supertrend below price: Uptrend.
- Red Supertrend above price: Downtrend.
- Simple and effective for identifying trend direction.

### Parabolic SAR
Trailing stop and reversal indicator.

- Dots below price: Uptrend.
- Dots above price: Downtrend.
- Useful for trailing stop-loss placement.

## Chart Patterns

### Candlestick Patterns
- **Doji**: Indecision — open and close nearly equal.
- **Hammer**: Bullish reversal at bottom of downtrend.
- **Engulfing**: Strong reversal when one candle engulfs the previous.
- **Morning/Evening Star**: Three-candle reversal patterns.

### Classic Patterns
- **Head and Shoulders**: Bearish reversal pattern.
- **Double Top/Bottom**: Reversal at key levels.
- **Triangle (ascending/descending/symmetrical)**: Continuation patterns.
- **Flag and Pennant**: Short-term continuation patterns.

## Support and Resistance

Support is a price level where buying pressure prevents further decline. Resistance is where selling pressure prevents further advance.

- **Key levels** are identified by previous highs, lows, and consolidation zones.
- **Fibonacci retracement** levels (23.6%, 38.2%, 50%, 61.8%) are common support/resistance areas.
- **Round numbers** often act as psychological support/resistance.
- Support becomes resistance when broken, and vice versa.
