# Order Flow Analysis

## CVD (Cumulative Volume Delta)
- CVD = sum of (market buy volume - market sell volume) over time
- Rising CVD → aggressive buying, bullish
- Falling CVD → aggressive selling, bearish
- CVD divergence with price → trend exhaustion signal
- CVD rising while price flat → accumulation

## Footprint Charts
- Shows bid/ask volume at each price level
- **Delta** = buyer-initiated volume - seller-initiated volume at each level
- **POC** (Point of Control) = price level with highest volume
- **VA** (Value Area) = price region containing 70% of volume

## Absorption / Exhaustion Patterns
- **Buying climax** — large delta at high prices, cannot sustain, reversal down
- **Selling climax** — large delta at low prices, cannot sustain, reversal up
- **Absorption** — large volume at key S/R level without price breaking through
- **Iceberg orders** — hidden large orders visible through footprint patterns

## Delta Divergence
- Price making higher highs, delta making lower highs → bearish divergence
- Price making lower lows, delta making higher lows → bullish divergence
- Most reliable on 1-15 minute charts for scalping
- Significant on 1H-4H for swing trading
