# Correlation Analysis

## BTC Dominance Cycle
- BTC dominance rising → capital rotating from altcoins to BTC (risk-off for alts)
- BTC dominance falling → altcoin season, capital flowing to high-beta altcoins
- Dominance tends to rise during bear markets (flight to safety)
- Dominance falls during bull market peaks (speculation spreads to alts)

## Alt Season Indicators
- Top 50 altcoins outperforming BTC over rolling 90 days
- Stablecoin inflows to exchanges → fuel for alt buying
- ETH/BTC pair rising → alt season typically follows ETH strength

## Cross-Asset Correlations
| Asset Pair | Typical Correlation | Note |
|---|---|---|
| BTC / S&P 500 | 0.3-0.6 (rising) | Increasing macro correlation |
| BTC / Gold | -0.2 to 0.3 | Low, sometimes inverse (digital gold narrative) |
| BTC / DXY | -0.3 to -0.5 | Inverse: strong USD → weak BTC |
| ETH / BTC | 0.6-0.8 | Directionally aligned, beta varies |

## Stablecoin Flows
- USDT/USDC minting → capital entering crypto
- Stablecoin exchange reserves → buying power
- Rising stablecoin dominance → risk-off within crypto

## Macro Correlations
- **Fed policy** → rate hikes → crypto sell-off (risk-asset repricing)
- **Liquidity cycles** → global M2 rising → crypto tends to rise
- **Inflation** → both hedge narrative (BTC) and risk-off (correlation shift)
